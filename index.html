GradeGauge

Student exam score predictor. Move the sliders and GradeGauge predicts the exam score (0–100) with a ridge regression model trained on 500 synthetic student records. Runs entirely in your browser — no backend, no data leaves this page.

—

How it works

Features are standardized with the training means and standard deviations, then a ridge regression (alpha = 1.0) predicts the score, clipped to 0–100. The strongest drivers are study hours and previous score, followed by attendance.

Results

  • 5-fold cross-validated R² = 0.871
  • Mean absolute error = 4.54 marks (vs 13.94 for predicting the class average — about 3× better)
  • RMSE = 5.81 marks on 500 synthetic records

Educational demo: predictions are estimates from synthetic training data, not a guarantee of any real exam result. Study habits matter — but so do revision quality, health and exam conditions.
